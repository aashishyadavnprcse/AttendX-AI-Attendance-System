Architecture Capabilities

Seven Pillars of Modern Attendance Intelligence

AI Face Biometrics

YuNet deep neural multi-face detection combined with SFace 128-dimensional biometric embeddings. Accurate identity matching with sub-second inference.

Anti-Spoof Defense

High-frequency texture analysis, moiré screening, specular reflection scoring, and eye-blink tracking to block paper photos, tablet replay, and digital deepfakes.

Automated Absent SMS

Automatically evaluates class attendance upon session conclusion. Dispatches instant SMS notices to absent students and parents with duplicate suppression.

Campus Geofencing & BLE

Enforces physical presence within a strict 20-meter classroom perimeter via HTML5 GPS telemetry and Log-Distance BLE beacon proximity RSSI validation.

Tamper-Evident SHA-256

Attendance records form a forward cryptographic hash chain. Any retrospective database tampering is instantly flagged during cryptographic ledger verification.

Continuous Presence

Classroom timeline tracking measures genuine engagement throughout the lecture duration, distinguishing students who attend the full session from early leavers.
